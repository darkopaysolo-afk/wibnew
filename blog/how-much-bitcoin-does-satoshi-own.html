How much Bitcoin does Satoshi own?

Why the familiar “one million BTC” figure is an estimate, and why a mining pattern is not a verified wallet balance.

Reviewed October 29, 2026 · By WhoInventedBitcoin.com · Editorial policy

There is no verified personal balance

Bitcoin records transactions, not a registry of legal identities. Looking at early block rewards cannot directly reveal the name of the person who received them. Any total attributed to Satoshi therefore needs a method and an explanation of its limits.

Where the large estimates came from

In 2013, Sergio Demian Lerner used patterns in early mining data to associate blocks with a presumed common miner. His published count identified approximately 980,000 BTC in the selected pattern.

In 2018, BitMEX Research revisited the approach. It argued that a dominant miner was plausible but that the attribution was less robust than often assumed, suggesting a lower estimate of roughly 600,000–700,000 BTC.

Two uncertainties are being combined

First, which blocks belong to the same miner? Second, was that miner Satoshi? Resolving one does not automatically resolve the other. Quoting a total without both caveats turns a research hypothesis into an apparent account statement.

Why “Satoshi's wallet” can mislead

Early rewards were distributed across transaction outputs, rather than collected in a single publicly verified personal account. Wallet software can manage many keys, and a collection of attributed outputs is different from a wallet whose ownership has been established.

What about a dollar value?

Multiplying an estimated coin count by a market price creates an estimated valuation with two changing assumptions. It does not establish that the owner has access to those coins, could sell them all at that price, or is a particular person.

When reading a fortune headline, look for the research date, the block attribution method, and the distinction between observation and inference. We do not claim to monitor these outputs continuously or verify their present spending status.

Check the sources

  1. Sergio Demian Lerner — early mining analysis (2013)
  2. BitMEX Research — Does Satoshi have a million bitcoin? (2018)
  3. Bitcoin Developer Guide — Block chain
