Why did Satoshi Nakamoto disappear?

The April 2011 emails document a departure. They do not establish the reason behind it.

Reviewed October 29, 2026 · By WhoInventedBitcoin.com · Editorial policy

The clearest evidence is ordinary correspondence

On April 23, 2011, Satoshi told developer Mike Hearn that they had moved on and expressed confidence in Gavin and the other contributors. Hearn published the exchange. The message also encouraged further development of BitcoinJ, a Java client.

That context matters. The note is about continued work by other people, not a confession of identity or a detailed explanation of personal circumstances. It supports a narrow conclusion: Satoshi was stepping back while encouraging the project to continue.

Departure and motive are different questions

It is tempting to turn silence into a story. Perhaps Satoshi wanted privacy, thought the project no longer needed a founder, or simply had other priorities. These are possible interpretations. The email does not choose between them.

A useful way to read any explanation is to ask what observation could distinguish it from the alternatives. If several incompatible stories all fit the same short message, that message cannot prove any one of them.

Why the wording is often misreported

The familiar departure note mentions Gavin, but it was addressed to Hearn. Quoting it without its recipient and date makes separate conversations easy to confuse. It should also not be presented as proof of Satoshi's final communication to anyone.

What remains unknown

The archive does not tell us where Satoshi went or whether the departure was intended to be permanent. It cannot establish whether later silence reflects choice, lost access, or personal circumstances.

Our interpretation is that the handover matters more to Bitcoin's history than an invented dramatic ending. Software can continue through public contributions even when its original author stops participating. That observation explains why the project could outlast the pseudonym without solving the mystery behind it.
