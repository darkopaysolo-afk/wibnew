Craig Wright and the Satoshi question

The High Court of England and Wales rejected Wright’s claim in 2024. This is a documented ruling, not an unresolved candidate ranking.

Reviewed October 29, 2026 · By WhoInventedBitcoin.com · Editorial policy

CW
Evidence status

Court ruled he is not Satoshi

What did the court decide?

In COPA v Wright, the High Court of England and Wales determined that Craig Wright was not Satoshi Nakamoto, did not author the Bitcoin whitepaper, did not create Bitcoin, and did not author the initial Bitcoin software.

Why this differs from other theories

The May 2024 judgment examines the evidence presented in litigation, including documents relied on to support Wright's claim. The court found extensive dishonesty and forgery.

How we describe his claim

Wright belongs in the history of claims about Satoshi, with the ruling stated prominently. Presenting him as merely a controversial candidate would omit the central outcome of the case.

Read the decision

The linked judgment is the source for this summary. It provides the court's reasoning, rather than a popularity vote or a collection of online opinions.
