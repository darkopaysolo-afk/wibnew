Who was Hal Finney?

Finney worked on PGP and experimented with reusable proof of work before Bitcoin. In his own 2013 account, he described downloading the early software, reporting bugs, and receiving ten coins from Satoshi.

Why people connect him to Satoshi

His background fits the technical world from which Bitcoin emerged. But possessing relevant skills and participating early are different from demonstrating authorship.

What points the other way?

Finney's account describes Satoshi as someone he corresponded with. That is a primary account of collaboration, rather than an admission of a second identity. Any theory that treats the two people as one has to explain that record.

Our assessment

Important early contributor; identity unconfirmed. Read Finney's account before accepting a headline that turns capability into proof. His documented contribution matters regardless of who Satoshi was.
