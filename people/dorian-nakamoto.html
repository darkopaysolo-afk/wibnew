Dorian Nakamoto and the Satoshi question

A shared name is not evidence of authorship. Dorian publicly rejected the identification made in 2014.

Reviewed October 29, 2026 · By WhoInventedBitcoin.com · Editorial policy

DN
Evidence status

Denied identification; identity not established

What is the distinction?

Dorian Satoshi Nakamoto is a real person whose name appeared in reporting about Bitcoin's creator. Sharing a name with a pseudonym does not establish a connection to the software.

His response

In a March 2014 statement issued through his lawyer, Dorian denied creating or working on Bitcoin.

How to evaluate this claim

The question is whether there is independently verifiable evidence of authorship. A matching name, a photograph, or a suggestive coincidence is insufficient. Repeating an identification many times does not supply the missing evidence.

Our assessment

Identification denied; authorship not established. We include this profile to explain the historical claim, not to present personal details as clues. There is no need to investigate someone's home or family to understand Bitcoin.
