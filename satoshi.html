Who is Satoshi Nakamoto?

Satoshi is the pseudonym credited with creating Bitcoin. No real-world identity has been publicly established. Explore the documents and competing theories.

Reviewed October 29, 2026 · By WhoInventedBitcoin.com · Editorial policy

Begin with what can be checked

The Bitcoin whitepaper names Satoshi Nakamoto as its author. The name itself does not establish a biography. Our profiles distinguish documented contributions, circumstantial clues, and claims rejected in court.

A departure, not an explanation

In an April 23, 2011 email to Mike Hearn, Satoshi said they had moved on and encouraged continued development. The message mentioned Gavin; it was not addressed to him. It does not establish why Satoshi left.

Read the departure story →

What about the fortune?

Large holdings attributed to Satoshi are estimates based on early mining patterns. The total and the miner’s identity are debated. There is no single verified “Satoshi wallet” containing one million BTC.

Could Satoshi have been a group?

The pseudonym could in principle represent one person or several people. Breadth of expertise alone does not settle that question. We treat it as an open possibility, not an established explanation.
